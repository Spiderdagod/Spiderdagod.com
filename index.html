The road to Godfall

2018: “Dreamkillers.” 2026: “Celebration.”

“Dreamkillers” was the first single from a project called Godfall. It was about the people closest to you who try to kill the dream.

Eight years, two albums and a run of features later, “Celebration” is the payoff: growth, business, and work that finally paid off.

Lyrical East Coast rap out of Buffalo, on records with Conway, Keith Murray, M.O.P., Termanology and K-Rino.
